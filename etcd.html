etcd's trace validation

etcd's raft library logs its state transitions and checks each log against its TLA⁺ spec with TLC (tla/validate.sh): the spec follows the trace, and TraceMatched fails where it cannot. make etcd-bench logs a trace from each of the library's interaction tests, with Go, and checks each, and the trace etcd ships, with manyan and with TLC as validate.sh runs it. Every difference in what they print is a bug.

etcd-io/raft at 99f9ffb3d19c: the traces its interaction tests log, and tla/example.ndjson. Measured on arm64 Darwin 25.5.0, one run, wall-clock seconds, one worker.
