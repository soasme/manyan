Full benchmark

make full-bench checks the models of tlaplus/Examples with Manyan and with TLC, and writes this report. make bench (benchmark) is the quick one for day-to-day work; this one takes hours and asks a stronger question: does Manyan print what TLC prints on specs it was not written for?

Examples, CommunityModules and TLAPS are pinned in the Makefile. Each model runs once with one worker, killed past 15 minutes (TIMEOUT= seconds), 4 GB held, swapped out too, or once the disk has under 4 GB free, as TLC's states spilled to disk can leave it. TLC's results are cached under .obj, a run killed for time run again under a longer TIMEOUT, and the diffs of the models that differ are written to .obj/fullbench.diff. ONLY=DieHard runs the models whose path contains DieHard; MAX_RUNTIME=00:00:30 keeps those recorded to run in 30 seconds, and MAX_RUNTIME=all takes every model.

Of Examples' 243 models, the report below checks all 191 that TLC searches exhaustively, with MAX_RUNTIME=all TIMEOUT=3600, and the 6 that simulate or generate behaviors, fixed by -seed 1 and num=; the other 46 are Apalache's. Where TLC is killed, there is nothing to compare. Five differ, for reasons Manyan keeps:

  • ewd687a/EWD687a_anim: its ALIAS lays out its graph with SVG's NodesOfDirectedMultiGraph, which TLC computes with jungrapht's Sugiyama layout, a Java library's heuristics, and Manyan from its definition, a CHOOSE it cannot enumerate. Copying a layout library for one animation is not planned.
  • SpanningTree/SpanTreeRandom draws its graph with RandomElement in a constant, which TLC draws anew each run, so its output is TLC's of one run.
  • FiniteMonotonic/MCDistributedReplicatedLog: the same property is violated, but under its VIEW the two print different traces (tlaplus#1045).
  • tower_of_hanoi/Hanoi.toolbox/Model_1/MC: TLC loads the model's own Bits.class and says so; Manyan computes Bits!And from its definition, with the same result.
  • ewd998/SmokeEWD998_SC runs TLC itself, through IOEnvExec and the path TLCGet("config").install gives of TLC's jar, which Manyan has none of.

The four others the last report held over 4 GB print what TLC prints, ElevatorSafetyLarge's 59M states at a peak footprint of 3854 MB and aba_asyn_byz's three temporal properties at 3794 MB, near the guard's 4000.
  • More than one worker: the 167 models recorded to run in a minute or less were run once with -workers 4 (by an engine wrapping manyan -workers 4): 160 print what TLC prints, as with one worker, and the four that differ with one worker differ alike; the run found TLCSailfish1's parse failure, since fixed. SlushMedium and MCBoulanger went past the 4 GB guard, which summed the resident memory of the worker processes, pages they share counted in each; it counts them once now, and run again with four workers both print what TLC prints, SlushMedium in 17.45 s (10.1x) and MCBoulanger in 16.64 s (9.6x), holding 2.1 and 1.3 GB, where one worker holds 1.9 and 1.2.
